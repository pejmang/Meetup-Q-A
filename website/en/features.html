AFTER · Extend

Don’t leave questions unanswered.

Revisit contributions, identify open topics and prepare the next answers to keep the conversation going.

  • See which questions were answered and which were not.
  • Review the priorities your audience expressed.
  • Prepare the follow-up and the next discussions.
